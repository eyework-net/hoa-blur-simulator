Educational optical blur simulator

Higher-Order Aberration Blur Simulator

This tool converts ocular aberration values into an intuitive visual blur simulation. It is intended for clinical explanation, education, and demonstration rather than exact Zernike-based optical reconstruction.

Input Parameters

Adjust pupil size and aberration values. Effects are scaled visually for educational use.
4.0 mm
Larger pupils increase the visible impact of aberrations, especially in dim light.
0.30 μm
Controls the global blur and contrast reduction.
0.15 μm
Produces directional smearing or comet-like tailing around edges and lights.
0.10 μm
Creates three-directional ghosting or tri-radial spread.
0.05 μm
Adds image displacement and asymmetric offset.

Clinical Comment

Symptoms may be subtle under photopic conditions and more noticeable in dim light.

Important limitation: This simulator does not calculate a true point spread function from full Zernike coefficients. It uses simplified visual transformations based on total aberration, coma, trefoil, tilt, and pupil size. It should be used for education and communication, not for diagnostic decision-making or quantitative optical evaluation.
